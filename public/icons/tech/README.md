# Technology icons

Brand SVG paths are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), distributed under CC0. They are styled in graphite to match the portfolio. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.

The queue, events, PDF-table, and document-check icons are authored unbranded symbols for BullMQ, Inngest, pdfplumber, and Docling. These are deliberately not presented as official logos. Technology labels always remain visible alongside the icons.

The mapping is editable in `src/data/technologies.js`.
