# Goutam portfolio video prompt

Use [the six pose storyboard](../public/storyboard/goutam-video-storyboard.png) as the visual reference image for an image-to-video model. The uploaded portrait established Goutam’s identity; the storyboard carries that identity, outfit, and pose sequence into the video.

## Prompt

Create one continuous, polished 8-second 2D anime editorial animation from the attached six-panel storyboard. It is the opening film for Goutam Kumar Sharma’s software developer portfolio. Preserve the same recognizable young South Asian man, face, short black hair, subtle facial hair, warm brown hoodie, charcoal cargo trousers, proportions, line work, and vermilion cricket ball in every moment.

Keep a pure, evenly lit white studio background throughout. Frame Goutam center-right with generous clean white negative space across the left 42 percent for website copy. Use a steady wide camera with only a gentle push-in and slight lateral drift. Keep his whole head, shoulders, chest, both arms, and the ball comfortably in frame until the final throw. Make the hand and ball motion smooth, physically connected, and readable between poses.

Timing: 0–1.2 seconds, Goutam smiles and points to himself at his chest while holding the ball; 1.2–2.5 seconds, casually tosses and tracks the ball with his eyes; 2.5–3.8 seconds, opens his arms and gestures naturally toward his forearm and chest as if describing how he works; 3.8–4.9 seconds, turns slightly and brings his hands together, then opens them to suggest separate ideas connecting; 4.9–6.0 seconds, winds up with the ball and looks toward the viewer; 6.0–7.1 seconds, throws the ball toward the camera with a strong but believable follow-through; 7.1–8.0 seconds, the ball bursts into a small, elegant fan of paper project cards that settle in the white space. End on a clean composition that can dissolve into a project gallery.

Maintain the same character model, outfit, light, white background, and camera across the full shot. Preserve clear silhouette separation from the background. Render at 16:9, 24 fps, with no text baked into the image.

## Avoid

No masks, gradient veils, vignettes, color washes, scenery, extra people, outfit or face changes, camera cuts, jump cuts, duplicate limbs, warped fingers, ball teleporting, flicker, trails over the face, logos, captions, interface screenshots, watermarks, or excessive particles. Keep the white stage clean and the left copy area unobstructed for the entire film.
